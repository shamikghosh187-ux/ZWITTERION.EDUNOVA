Meet SmartScan.

Turn scanned pages into editable text and export a clean typed PDF or a layout-inspired PDF. OCR is performed with Google Cloud Vision from a protected server endpoint. Review every page before exporting.

01 · Add scanned pages

Choose JPG, PNG or WebP images from the existing scanner or your device. Use clear, upright pages for best results.

Use the document camera or upload scans
Capture multiple pages with the rear camera, or choose existing images.
Privacy: page images are sent to Google Cloud Vision for OCR and are not stored by this SmartScan endpoint. Do not upload documents without the appropriate permission.

02 · Review and export

OCR text from each page appears below. Correct recognition mistakes before creating the final document.

Reflow text into a clean, readable document with page headings.

Layout-inspired means readable typed text with page breaks and preserved line order where possible. It cannot perfectly reproduce the original placement of diagrams, equations, or handwriting.
ZWITTERION SmartScan · OCR can make mistakes, especially with handwriting and equations. Always review before sharing.
