SmartScan → PDF

Turn scanned printed questions and handwritten answers into editable text, review the recognition, then export a clean PDF or a page-organized PDF.

Use clear, upright images. For best results, keep each image under 8 MB. This first version accepts images, not multi-page PDF uploads.
OCR may misread handwriting, equations, or diagrams. Check and correct the text before generating the final PDF. Text extraction does not automatically identify which lines are questions versus answers.

Your chosen images are sent to Google Cloud Vision for OCR. Avoid uploading sensitive personal information. This tool does not grade answers.
